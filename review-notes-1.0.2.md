APP REVIEW NOTES — Together: Men's Mental Health

No login or account is required. The app opens directly to full
functionality — you can start using it immediately without any
credentials. Please leave the demo account fields blank / "Sign-in
not required".

=== WHAT'S NEW IN THIS VERSION ===

This update adds dark mode, haptic feedback, a redesigned breathing
exercise, manual data backup/restore, and a fix to daily reminder
scheduling. It also completes full Norwegian localization alongside
English. No changes to account handling, subscriptions, or community
moderation are included in this update.

=== COMMUNITY / USER-GENERATED CONTENT ===

The anonymous Community feature requires accepting our community
guidelines before it can be viewed or posted to (shown on first visit
to the Community tab). An automated content filter blocks objectionable
posts at submission time. Every post has a "⋯ More" menu with
Report / Block / Hide. Posts that receive multiple reports are
automatically hidden pending review. Reports are reviewed and
actioned within 24 hours. Users can manage blocked users and report
issues under Profile → Safety & community. In-app contact for abuse
reports: together@cliffordcoaching.no. Age rating is set to 18+.

Test path: Community tab → accept guidelines → tap "⋯ More" on any
post to see Report / Block / Hide.

=== SUBSCRIPTION ===

There is a single subscription product: Together Pro Monthly
(com.kricliff.together.pro.monthly), $4.99/month. The paywall
displays price, billing period, auto-renewal terms, a Restore
Purchases button, and links to our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use
(EULA) before purchase.

Test path: Profile tab → "Go Pro" (or tap any Pro-locked padlock
elsewhere in the app).

=== CONTACT ===

If anything fails to load during review, it is most likely a
temporary network issue with the subscription or community service —
please retry, or contact us at together@cliffordcoaching.no and we
will respond promptly.
