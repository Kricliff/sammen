Support

Need help with the app? You're in the right place.

Have a question, found a bug, or just want to share feedback? Email us at together@cliffordcoaching.no and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

How do I restore my Pro purchase?
Go to Profile → Go Pro → Restore Purchases. This works as long as you're signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

How do I back up or restore my data?
Go to Profile → Your data. You can create a manual backup and use it to restore your entries if you delete or reinstall the app, or move to a new device.

How do I report an inappropriate Community post?
Tap "⋯ More" on any post and choose Report, Block, or Hide. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours. You can also reach us directly at the email above.

How do I change the app's language?
Go to Profile → Settings → Language, and choose English or Norwegian.

In a crisis

Together is a tool, not a replacement for professional help. If you're in crisis, please reach out to a professional or call your local crisis line. The app's Crisis support button (top of every screen) has quick access to help.
