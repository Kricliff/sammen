APP REVIEW NOTES — Together: Men's Mental Health

No login or account is required. The app opens directly to full
functionality — you can start using it immediately without any
credentials. Please leave the demo account fields blank / "Sign-in
not required".

=== WHAT'S NEW IN THIS VERSION ===

This is a content update. It adds two new self-guided programmes
("30 small steps", 30 days, and "Through the dark season", 14 days),
a sleep-and-mood insight card on the Progress tab (it compares the
user's own logged mood on shorter vs longer nights, calculated on
device; no data leaves the phone), and a set of Community discussion
prompts used during November, the month for men's health. During
November a card on the Home tab invites the user to start the
"30 small steps" programme.

The programme content is general wellbeing guidance (daylight, sleep
routine, movement, social contact, booking a GP check-up). It is not
medical advice, and the dark-season programme explicitly tells users
to see their GP if low mood lasts two weeks or more.

Test path: Training tab → Programmes → "30 small steps" or "Through
the dark season" → Start. The new insight card is on Progress →
Overview (log sleep from the Home tab and mood on a few days to see
it fill in).

=== COMMUNITY / USER-GENERATED CONTENT ===

Unchanged from the previous version. The anonymous Community requires
accepting our community guidelines before viewing or posting. An
automated content filter blocks objectionable posts and replies at
submission time. Every post and reply has Report / Block / Hide.
Content with multiple reports is automatically hidden pending review,
and reports are actioned within 24 hours. Users manage blocked users
under Profile → Safety & community. Contact for abuse reports:
together@cliffordcoaching.no. Age rating is 18+.

Test path: Community tab → accept guidelines → "⋯ More" on any post.

=== IN-APP PURCHASES ===

All features in this build are free. The app does not present a
paywall or initiate any in-app purchase.

=== CONTACT ===

If anything fails to load during review, it is most likely a
