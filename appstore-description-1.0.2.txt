Most mental health apps aren't built for men. Together is.

No journalling prompts that feel like therapy homework. No passive meditation streaks. Just practical tools, real community, and the kind of straightforward support that actually fits how men think.

Available in English and Norwegian, with full dark mode support.

WHAT TOGETHER OFFERS

— Daily check-in
Log your mood in seconds. See patterns emerge over time. No pressure, no judgment.

— Guided exercises
Short, effective mental training: breathing techniques, grounding exercises, and reflections designed to fit into a real day.

— Training programmes
Structured 7–30 day programmes covering confidence, fatherhood, life transitions, anger, and more. Start one. Or several.

— Community
An anonymous space to share what's actually going on. Reply to posts, read what other men are carrying, and get notified when someone responds. You're not the only one.

— Learn
Research-backed articles on men's mental health — loneliness, burnout, identity, relationships — written without the fluff.

— Journal and weekly review
Private space to think on paper, with your history right there when you want to look back. A structured weekly reflection, with an optional reminder to close the week with intention.

ABOUT THE DEVELOPER

Together is built by Kristian Clifford, a certified coach and mental trainer with training in performance psychology and mental training. This app is a tool — not a replacement for professional help. If you're in crisis, please reach out to a professional or call your local crisis line.

PRIVACY

All your personal data — moods, journal entries, logs — is stored only on your device. We collect nothing. You can back up and restore your data manually at any time, and a background safety-net backup keeps a copy while the app is installed. See our full privacy policy at cliffordcoaching.no/privacy.
