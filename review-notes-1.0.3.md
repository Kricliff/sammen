APP REVIEW NOTES — Together: Men's Mental Health

No login or account is required. The app opens directly to full
functionality — you can start using it immediately without any
credentials. Please leave the demo account fields blank / "Sign-in
not required".

=== WHAT'S NEW IN THIS VERSION ===

This update reorganizes navigation: Training and Learn are now one tab
(Exercises / Programmes / Learn sub-sections), and a new Progress tab
holds mood history, journal, weekly reflection, and the user's own
Community post history, all previously scattered across Home and
Profile. Profile itself is now settings-only (account, notifications,
subscription, safety, data backup). Mood check-in now has 5 levels
instead of 4, and weekly review also shows sleep and exercise activity
logged that week, not just mood.

It also adds reply threads to Community posts (covered by the same
content filter and Report/Block/Hide moderation as top-level posts,
plus an in-app notification when someone replies to your post), a
Norwegian-language contact form for booking a call with the app's
coach, an optional weekly reflection reminder, a background
safety-net data backup, and a fix to daily reminder scheduling. It
also updates the paywall to display free-trial terms when a trial is
configured. No changes to account handling or the core subscription
product are included in this update.

=== COMMUNITY / USER-GENERATED CONTENT ===

The anonymous Community feature requires accepting our community
guidelines before it can be viewed or posted to (shown on first visit
to the Community tab). An automated content filter blocks objectionable
posts at submission time, and the same filter applies to replies.
Every post and reply has a "⋯ More" / report control with
Report / Block / Hide. Posts or replies that receive multiple reports
are automatically hidden pending review. Reports are reviewed and
actioned within 24 hours. Users can manage blocked users and report
issues under Profile → Safety & community. In-app contact for abuse
reports: together@cliffordcoaching.no. Age rating is set to 18+.

Test path: Community tab → accept guidelines → open any post to reply
→ tap the report control on a reply to see Report / Block / Hide.

=== SUBSCRIPTION ===

There is a single subscription product: Together Pro Monthly
(com.kricliff.together.pro.monthly), $4.99/month, with a 3-day free
trial for new subscribers. The paywall displays price, billing period,
trial terms, auto-renewal terms, a Restore Purchases button, and links
to our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use (EULA) before purchase.

Test path: Profile tab → "Go Pro" (or tap any Pro-locked padlock
elsewhere in the app).

=== CONTACT ===

If anything fails to load during review, it is most likely a
temporary network issue with the subscription or community service —
please retry, or contact us at together@cliffordcoaching.no and we
will respond promptly.
