APP REVIEW NOTES — Together: Men's Mental Health

No login or account is required. The app opens directly to full
functionality — you can start using it immediately without any
credentials. Please leave the demo account fields blank / "Sign-in
not required".

=== WHAT'S NEW IN THIS VERSION ===

- Today's question: a daily discussion question in Community (a pool
  of 60, plus one question for each day of November, men's health
  month). It is also shown on the Home tab. Answering opens the normal
  Community composer, so all moderation below applies.
  Test path: Home tab → "Today's question" card → "Answer anonymously".

- Skip stones: a small, calm one-button game for taking a short break.
  No timer, no scoring against others, no ads, no purchases. Only the
  personal best is stored, on device.
  Test path: Training tab → Exercises → "Switch off" → "Skip stones".
  Hold anywhere on the screen, then let go.

- Share the app: a "Share Together" card at the top of the Profile tab,
  and a share button when a programme is completed. It opens the
  standard iOS share sheet with a short text and the App Store link.
  Nothing is sent unless the user chooses a destination.

- Ratings: the app uses Apple's own review prompt
  (SKStoreReviewController). It is requested only after 7 days of mood
  check-ins in a row on a day logged as "Okay" or better, or after
  completing a programme, and never more than once every 120 days.
  There is no custom rating dialog. A "Rate Together in the App Store"
  link in Profile opens the App Store page on request.

- Cloud backup control: Profile → Your data → "Automatic cloud backup".
  The backup (stored in Firebase under an anonymous ID) is on by
  default; turning it off stops backups and deletes the stored copy.
  The privacy policy has been updated to describe this.

- Clearer wording throughout the English text.

=== COMMUNITY / USER-GENERATED CONTENT (Guideline 1.2) ===

Unchanged from the previous version. The anonymous Community requires
accepting our community guidelines before viewing or posting. An
automated content filter blocks objectionable posts and replies at
submission time, including answers to Today's question. Every post
and reply has Report / Block / Hide. Content with multiple reports is
automatically hidden pending review, and reports are actioned within
24 hours. Users manage blocked users under Profile → Safety &
community. Contact for abuse reports: together@cliffordcoaching.no.
Age rating is 18+.

Test path: Community tab → accept guidelines → "⋯ More" on any post.

=== IN-APP PURCHASES ===

All features in this build are free. The app does not present a
paywall or initiate any in-app purchase.

=== CONTACT ===

If anything fails to load during review, it is most likely a
