=== FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000 chars) ===
Most mental health apps aren't built for men. Together is.

Practical tools, real community, and straightforward support that actually fits how men think. No therapy homework, no streaks to keep up. Everything is free.

WHAT'S INSIDE

- Daily mood check-ins that take seconds
- Guided exercises for stress, anger, panic, sleep and hard moments
- Structured programmes on confidence, fatherhood, anxiety, loneliness, grief and more
- An anonymous Community to share what's really going on, with replies and support
- Your progress in one place: mood trends, how your sleep affects your mood, journal and weekly review
- Research-backed articles on men's mental health

Available in English and Norwegian, with full dark mode support.

Built by Kristian Clifford, a certified coach and mental trainer. Together is a tool, not a replacement for professional help. If you're in crisis, contact a professional or call your local crisis line.

Your moods, journal and logs are private. They're stored on your device, with an anonymous backup so you don't lose them. No account, no name, no email. We use anonymous, aggregated usage statistics to improve the app, never for advertising or tracking. Privacy policy: kricliff.github.io/sammen/privacy.html
